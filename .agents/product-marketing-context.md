# Product Marketing Context

*Last updated: 2026-03-17*

## Product Overview
**One-liner:** Bitcoin standard advisory, writing, and community signal from Pavao Pahljina.
**What it does:** This practice shares practical Bitcoin-standard ideas, tools, and money habits, while also offering advisory support and community guidance. The website acts as a central hub for writing, live conversations, public profiles, and projects connected to Bitcoin education and community building.
**Product category:** Bitcoin advisory and education
**Product type:** Personal advisory practice and media hub
**Business model:** Relationship-driven advisory, writing, community building, and call booking via Cal.com

## Target Audience
**Target companies:** Bitcoin-curious individuals, operators, entrepreneurs, and community builders
**Decision-makers:** Individuals seeking guidance, collaborators, and community organizers
**Primary use case:** Find credible Bitcoin-standard guidance, writing, and points of contact
**Jobs to be done:**
- Understand how to move toward living on a Bitcoin standard
- Find trusted writing, communities, and projects worth following
- Reach out for direct conversation, support, or collaboration
**Use cases:**
- Evaluate Pavao's advisory and educational work
- Explore Practical Bitcoin Standard and related projects
- Book a call or email for direct contact

## Problems & Pain Points
**Core problem:** People interested in Bitcoin often struggle to find practical, trustworthy guidance that connects ideas, habits, tools, and communities.
**Why alternatives fall short:** Many sources are either too abstract, too noisy, too speculative, or disconnected from real-world implementation and strong communities.
**What it costs them:** Lost time, poor decisions, weak signal, and slower movement toward a Bitcoin-first life.
**Emotional tension:** Uncertainty, skepticism, information overload, and fear of wasting energy on the wrong people or resources.

## Competitive Landscape
**Direct:** Other Bitcoin advisors, educators, and writers — often fall short by being too generic or not practically grounded.
**Secondary:** Bitcoin podcasts, newsletters, and online personalities — useful, but often fragmented or not action-oriented.
**Indirect:** Traditional financial advice, mainstream media, or general business communities — often misaligned with Bitcoin-standard thinking.

## Differentiation
**Key differentiators:**
- Practical Bitcoin-standard framing
- Combination of advisory, writing, and community work
- Credibility across both global and Balkan Bitcoin networks
- Direct connection to real projects, livestreams, and communities
**How we do it differently:** The work combines lived experience, public writing, active project involvement, and direct community participation.
**Why that's better:** Visitors get a more grounded path from ideas to action instead of disconnected content or pure commentary.
**Why customers choose us:** They want signal, practicality, and a credible human point of contact.

## Switching Dynamics
**Push:** Frustration with low-signal Bitcoin content and abstract advice.
**Pull:** Practical guidance, strong communities, and a clear human contact point.
**Habit:** Staying with passive content consumption instead of taking action.
**Anxiety:** Worry that direct outreach or deeper involvement may not be worth the time.
